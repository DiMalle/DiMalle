### 🧭 A bit about my journey

I started out just curious about how websites worked, and that curiosity turned into a career. These days, I split my time between two builds close to my heart: an **audit management web app** and an **invoicing app** — both born from real problems I've seen people struggle with.

Somewhere along the way I picked up an interest in AI and large language models. Life (and a mountain of client work) pulled me away from it before I could really dig in — but it's still on my list, waiting for the right moment to pick back up.

When I'm not deep in code, I'm usually talking about whatever's new in tech — so feel free to pull me into that conversation.

- 💬 **Ask me about:** new trends in the tech industry
